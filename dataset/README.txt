Construction Materials Catalog
================================
Purpose:
A starter catalog for the AI-Based Smart Construction Management System.

Important:
- Prices are indicative reference values, not supplier quotations.
- Prices vary by city, supplier, quantity, brand and date.
- Product URLs are included only where a manufacturer/product page was identified.
- Verify current local prices before using for real purchasing or final project estimates.

Recommended use:
1. Material recommendation
2. Material comparison
3. Basic cost estimation
4. Search/filter by category, grade, size and application
5. Seed data for your database

Suggested future fields:
image_url, supplier_name, supplier_url, stock_status, GST, wastage_percent, carbon_footprint, IS_code, city, price_date.
